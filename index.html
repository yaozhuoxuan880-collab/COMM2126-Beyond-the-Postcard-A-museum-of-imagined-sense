A Museum of Imagined Senses

Most journeys begin long before we arrive. Sometimes, all it takes is a photograph.

A beautiful landscape can make us imagine the sounds of the wind, the warmth of water, or what it feels like to stand somewhere we have never been.

But where do these imagined sensations come from? From the photograph? From the place? Or from our own memories?

Explore five destinations, compare two perspectives on each, and discover what might remain beyond the frame. There are no right or wrong feelings.
